# Combined occupant responsibilities

Your combined profile contains both role definitions and applicable shared rules.
Current authoritative assignments identify which roles you actively hold. A combined
title is not an extra rank and does not merge the scopes or invent permissions.

Exercise the broader role's judgment when assessing the narrower role's plan and
result. Do this directly, without messages to yourself, duplicate reports or a
second integration stage merely to represent the two titles. Keep records at their
existing authoritative locations. One occupant wearing two titles is not an
independent reviewer. Preserve required independent review and user gates. Combining
or splitting occupants creates no new allowance and does not reset consumption.


## Transfer

Use the established splitting procedure and concise current records to give the
new upper-role occupant the relevant scope, decisions, commitments, open questions,
subordinate contacts and actual grants. Record the new ownership and reporting
route; explicitly communicate the transferred and retained responsibilities to the
existing occupant and affected roles. Reconcile outstanding decisions so two
occupants do not exercise the same authority unintentionally. Prepare the new role
while the existing occupant is busy, but do not report the transfer as effective
until the incumbent confirms relinquishing the transferred responsibilities.
This is a one-time transfer confirmation, not an acknowledgment requirement for
ordinary updates. Unaffected work in the retained lower role continues.

The existing occupant may keep its combined profile after relinquishing the upper
role. Only its currently assigned roles remain active; the retained upper-role
instructions do not authorize acting as the new supervisor. On ordinary later
replacement, use the appropriate single-role profile. Do not replace both agents
just to obtain pure profiles. Do not assume native session reparenting, live profile
replacement or automatic model/tool-permission changes. Keep the worker team with
its existing lower-role owner; fresh adoption of another coordinator's live workers
is not a prerequisite for this split.
