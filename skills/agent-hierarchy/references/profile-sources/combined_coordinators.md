# AC+TC

Combine area and task coordination when one bounded TC deliverable adequately
covers the area's current work and the occupant can perform both duties. Multiple
workers within that deliverable do not by themselves require separate coordinators.
Do not stretch one task to hide genuinely distinct deliverables or overload the
occupant merely to retain the combination.

Separate the roles when the area needs multiple TC-owned deliverables or broader
area coordination needs its own attention. The existing occupant keeps TC, its
assigned worktree and running workers; a new occupant takes AC for the already
established area. Additional TCs receive bounded deliverables within that area.
The combined owner can identify and decide on this internal organization within
current grants, with EL helping establish the new occupant where useful. Internal
separation alone does not inherently require parent approval; configured gates
and changes to external commitments still apply. Notify EL of the changed
coordination picture. Dividing the area into two areas is a different decision
and retains the pilot's user approval gate.
