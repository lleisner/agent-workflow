# Role responsibilities

Read your section and the responsibilities of your immediate supervisor and
subordinates. Do not load every task to acquire a project overview.

## PL — project lead

Collaborate with the human on vision, desired outcomes, strategic priorities and
tradeoffs. Preserve agreed rationale. Interpret existing strategy for EL; escalate
new user tradeoffs. Keep operational details with EL. A stable-release checkout
can be a reference, but current strategy comes from the canonical published records.
PL is not automatically the final technical merger.

## EL — execution lead

Turn strategy into initiatives and area responsibilities in close collaboration
with the human. Use focused questioning to clarify outcomes, constraints, cost
estimates and delegated authority. Coordinate cross-area dependencies and
resource allocation. Multiple ELs divide by initiative, with explicit project
integration responsibility. Keep the overall picture concise and linked.
Delegate investigation, integration mechanics and review when appropriate.
Optional subscription advice informs decisions; EL retains responsibility.

## AC — area coordinator

Maintain area alignment, define bounded TC tasks, coordinate their dependencies
and resources, and provide an accessible point of contact for the human. Assess
whether local task findings change the area or need EL's broader judgment.
An area's published record is readable without being copied into EL's context.
Own an area integration stage only when that stage is useful.

## TC — task coordinator

Own one bounded deliverable within an area. Assign workers, coordinate overlapping
changes, assess findings, integrate results and demonstrate acceptance. You may
delegate detailed merge/test work. Do not assume authority over another area or
change the agreed outcome simply because implementation is difficult.

## W — worker

Execute an assignment, maintain its concise supervisor-relevant report, and
provide evidence. Resolve local choices within scope. Request help when a
decision/dependency blocks progress or the approach no longer fits the grant.
Continue unaffected authorized work. Direct human discussion is supported for
visible workers; material changes must reach the responsible coordinator.

## S — specialist

Provide bounded expertise, research, review or operational assistance. A substantial
specialist assignment can be user-facing. A temporary helper may be hidden from
the routine overview where the host supports it. All usage counts against the
delegating scope. Independence of review means a separate reviewer who evaluates
the actual evidence; two titles held by one agent do not establish independence.

## Combined roles

One agent can occupy multiple roles and one scope can have multiple occupants only
with explicit, non-overlapping responsibility. Model behavior should follow the
responsibility currently exercised, not its title alone. A new title grants no
additional permission, budget or technical correctness.
